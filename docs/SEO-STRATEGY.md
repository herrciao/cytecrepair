# SEO Strategy — 5 Axis Head Repair

## Business outcome

Attract operators, maintenance teams, service partners, and distributors who have an active M21 or G30 failure and need an independent aftermarket assessment. Organic traffic is useful only when it builds qualified discovery and repair enquiries.

## Runtime goal

Publish at least one technically human-reviewed, high-intent M21 article per week and grow non-branded organic discovery and qualified repair enquiries.

### Leading indicators

- Five evidence-backed candidate topics researched per week.
- One search intent selected without cannibalizing an existing page.
- One approved article published per week.
- Every published page passes technical, legal, build, metadata, schema, sitemap, and internal-link checks.

### Lagging indicators

- Non-branded Google Search impressions and clicks.
- Number of landing pages receiving organic impressions.
- Queries/pages in positions 1–10 and 11–20.
- Organic assessment or contact submissions.
- Seven-, 28-, and 90-day performance per published page.

Use the first complete 28-day Search Console period as the baseline. Do not invent a percentage-growth target before the baseline exists.

## Topic architecture

1. **Pillar:** `/knowledge/cytec-m21`
2. **High-intent services:** rotary-union leakage, spindle repair/rebuild, hydraulic pressure issues.
3. **Symptom spokes:** what the operator sees and how to isolate the system.
4. **Maintenance spokes:** safe observations and prevention based on verified experience.
5. **Case studies:** anonymized, real evidence chains from symptom to finding, repair, and validation.

## Candidate selection score

Score each candidate from 0–3 for:

- Repair/commercial intent.
- M21/G30 relevance.
- Gap in the existing site.
- Availability of first-hand technical evidence.
- Internal-link contribution.

Subtract 0–3 for:

- Cannibalization risk.
- Unsupported-claim risk.
- Generic or informational-only intent.

Select one topic per weekly run. A lower-volume problem query with real repair intent can outrank a high-volume generic query in priority.

## Content quality rules

- Start from the user's observable symptom or diagnostic decision.
- Clearly separate observations, likely causes, confirmation steps, and repair options.
- State uncertainty honestly and request the data needed for assessment.
- Use real photos or measurements only when anonymized and verified.
- Link to the most relevant service path; do not force every article to every service page.
- Prefer updating an existing URL when the same intent is already covered.
- Keep the engineering-manual tone: calm, precise, structured, and non-alarmist.

## Measurement boundary

Rankings and traffic are feedback, not automatic proof of technical quality. Search Console data, conversions, indexing, and owner-reviewed technical accuracy must be evaluated together.
